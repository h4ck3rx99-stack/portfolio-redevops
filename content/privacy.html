Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

RE Dev Ops ("we", "us") runs redevops.in and its project subdomains. This page explains what personal information we collect through the website, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Cookie choices and storage

Necessary storage remembers your cookie choice. Optional analytics is disabled until you accept it. There are no marketing trackers in this studio website.

The first-party local storage record redevops-consent contains a schema version, necessary and analytics choices, a timestamp and expiry. It contains no identifying information. The product default is six calendar months; this is not a legal retention requirement. Expired or invalid records ask for a new choice. If browser storage is unavailable, analytics stays off. If storage fails during withdrawal, the reload adds analytics=off to the page URL so the tracker stays disabled.

Use Cookie settings in any footer to change your choice. Withdrawing analytics consent calls Microsoft Clarity’s consent API, removes accessible Clarity first-party cookies and reloads the page to stop the loaded tracker. This does not erase previously transmitted data or third-party cookies.

Fonts are hosted on this site. Calendly is not loaded until you choose to activate booking after its explanation; an external booking link is also available. Booking does not require analytics consent. WhatsApp opens only through an explicit link or send action.

What we collect

  • Contact form: Your name, email and message are used to prepare an enquiry. Direct delivery is not currently configured; the email fallback opens your email app only when you select it. The form keeps your input if sending fails.
  • WhatsApp chat: when you press send, WhatsApp opens with your message. Anything you send there is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under their own privacy policy. We do not capture WhatsApp drafts.
  • Calendly bookings: if you book a call, Calendly collects your name, email and chosen time under its own privacy policy and shares them with us so we can hold the meeting.
  • Email and phone: if you write or call us directly, we keep that conversation to follow up with you.
  • Usage analytics: With your permission, Microsoft Clarity records page interactions to help us understand use of the site. Enquiry fields are masked. Advertising storage is denied. No Clarity script or request is loaded before analytics consent.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't add you to marketing lists without asking.
  • We don't ask for sensitive information such as financial or government ID details through the website.

Who else handles your data

To run the site we rely on a few service providers, who process data on our behalf or under their own policies: Vercel (hosting), Microsoft Clarity (analytics), Calendly (call scheduling), WhatsApp (chat), the email and automation tools we use to receive and organise enquiries, and — when you use the website checker on our homepage — the public services allorigins.win, corsproxy.io and codetabs.com, which fetch the page you enter so your browser can read it. The checker sends the web address you enter. Providers also receive ordinary connection information when your browser contacts them. It does not capture contact-form drafts. Some of these providers may store data outside India.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't turn into a project are deleted within 12 months. If we work together, we keep project communication for as long as the project needs it and as required for legal and accounting purposes.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you;
  • correct or update it;
  • delete it, where we don't need to keep it for a legal reason;
  • stop using it for anything other than what you originally contacted us about.

Email hello@redevops.in with the subject "Privacy request" and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also block or clear cookies in your browser at any time; the site works without them.

Changes

If we change how we handle personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
