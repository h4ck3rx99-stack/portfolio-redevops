PixelWorld

One shared 2400 × 1350 pixel canvas for the whole internet. Everyone paints on the same grid, live, and it never resets.

Visit live site ↗ (opens in new tab) Start a similar project

The project

One shared pixel canvas for the whole internet. Everyone paints on the same 2400 × 1350 grid, live, with 254 colours, guide images to trace, a timelapse of the whole history, and a pixel bank that refills every 30 seconds. It installs as an app and opens offline.

What we built
A live, shared pixel canvas anyone can paint on from a browser or phone.
For
Our own studio product — free to use
The hard part
Keeping one canvas in sync for everyone at once, live, with fair limits so no single device or network can flood it.
Our role
Design, development and deployment

Features

  • Live painting with everyone online
  • 254 colours, a colour wheel and a pick tool
  • Guide images to trace, kept on your own device
  • Timelapse of the canvas's whole history
  • Who painted each pixel, with reporting
  • Installs as an app and opens offline
