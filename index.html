How a project runs

No surprises. Here's exactly what happens after you get in touch.

  1. Day 1

    Free discovery call

    A 30-minute call to understand your business, goals and budget. If we're not the right fit, we'll tell you — and point you somewhere better.

  2. Within 48 hours

    Clear scope & quote

    A written plan with features, timeline and a fixed price before any work starts. You know exactly what you're paying for.

  3. 2–4 weeks

    Build in weekly sprints

    You get a live preview link and a progress update every week, so you can give feedback while it's still easy to change things.

  4. Launch day

    Launch & handover

    We deploy, set up hosting and analytics, and walk you through everything. Then we stay on call for fixes and the next round of ideas.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

What services does RE Dev Ops offer?

We offer web development (React, Next.js), cross-platform mobile app development (Flutter, React Native), and cloud infrastructure & DevOps (AWS, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes).

How fast can RE Dev Ops launch a product?

We deliver functional MVPs in 14 to 30 days. We work in fast sprints with weekly builds and direct communication with you.

How is RE Dev Ops different from a traditional agency?

There are no account managers or hand-offs. You work directly with the founding team who design and write your code, which keeps costs low and delivery fast.

What technologies does RE Dev Ops specialize in?

React, Next.js, TypeScript, Node.js, Flutter, React Native, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, GCP, PostgreSQL, Redis, GraphQL, and serverless architectures.

How can I contact RE Dev Ops?

Email hello@redevops.in, call +91 8734000403, or use the contact form on our website. We typically respond within 6–12 hours.

Is RE Dev Ops based in India?

Yes, our team operates from India and serves clients worldwide. We work remotely across time zones with flexible communication.

Do you provide NFC cards and review stands outside Bangalore?

Not yet. Our NFC products — smart business cards, Google review stands, menu and table tags, and custom NFC tags — are currently available only in Bangalore, where we design, program and deliver them ourselves. Web, app and cloud work is available everywhere.
