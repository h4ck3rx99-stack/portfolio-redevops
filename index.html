FAQ

Before we begin.

How do we decide what to build?

We start with your goals, users and constraints, then agree the scope and deliverables before development.

How is a project priced?

We prepare a quote around the agreed scope. Changes to that scope are discussed before extra work begins.

Who owns the finished work?

Source code, domain and hosting ownership are covered in the project agreement, with a documented handover.

How will we communicate?

You work directly with the founding team. We agree how to share progress previews, feedback and decisions.

How long will it take?

Timing depends on scope, integrations and feedback. We agree a schedule during planning rather than promise the same window for every project.

Where do you work?

Our studio operates from India and collaborates remotely. NFC products are currently available only in Bangalore.
