06 FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Something else on your mind? hello@redevops.in

What does RE Dev Ops build?

Marketing sites and web apps (Next.js, Astro, React), cross-platform mobile apps (Flutter, React Native), and the cloud setup behind them — hosting, CI/CD, databases and monitoring on AWS, GCP or Vercel.

You're college students. Can you handle a real project?

Yes. We run RE Dev Ops alongside college, and we only take on as many projects as we can do properly. Our client work is live — thefermentformula.in is a good example — and every studio build on this page is a working app you can open.

How long does a project take?

A marketing site usually takes 2–3 weeks. A focused MVP takes 14–30 days. We send a working build every week so you always know where things stand.

How is this different from an agency?

There's no account manager in the middle. You talk directly to the two people designing and writing your code, on WhatsApp, email or a call. Our overhead is small, so our pricing is too.

Who owns the code?

You do. You get the full repository, the hosting accounts in your name, and a handover call so you're never locked in to us.

How do I get in touch?
